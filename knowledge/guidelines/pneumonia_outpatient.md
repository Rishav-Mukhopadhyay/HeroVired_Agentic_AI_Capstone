# Community-acquired pneumonia: outpatient adults
Source: 2019 ATS/IDSA Guideline for Diagnosis and Treatment of Adults with Community-acquired Pneumonia; educational summary.

## Outpatient treatment
- Healthy adults without comorbidities: amoxicillin 1 g three times daily, or doxycycline 100 mg twice daily, or a macrolide only where pneumococcal macrolide resistance is below 25%.
- With comorbidities (chronic heart, lung, liver or kidney disease, diabetes, alcohol use disorder, malignancy, asplenia): amoxicillin-clavulanate or a cephalosporin combined with a macrolide or doxycycline, or monotherapy with a respiratory fluoroquinolone.
Treat for at least 5 days and until clinically stable.

## Red flags
Confusion, respiratory rate 30 or more, low blood pressure, low oxygen saturation or age 65 and older with comorbidities: assess severity (for example CURB-65) and consider hospital care.
