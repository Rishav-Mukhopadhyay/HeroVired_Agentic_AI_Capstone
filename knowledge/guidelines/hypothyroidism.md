# Hypothyroidism
Source: American Thyroid Association Guidelines for the Treatment of Hypothyroidism; educational summary.

## Diagnosis
Raised TSH with low free T4 (overt) or normal free T4 (subclinical).

## Treatment
Levothyroxine. Full replacement dose is about 1.6 mcg/kg/day in healthy younger adults; start lower (for example 25-50 mcg, or 12.5-25 mcg) in older adults and those with coronary heart disease. Recheck TSH 6 to 8 weeks after any dose change.

## Practical points
Take on an empty stomach, 30-60 minutes before breakfast, separated by about 4 hours from calcium, iron and proton pump inhibitors which reduce absorption. Over-replacement risks atrial fibrillation and bone loss.
