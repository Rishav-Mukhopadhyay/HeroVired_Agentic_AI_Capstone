# Depression in primary care
Source: APA Practice Guideline for the Treatment of Patients with Major Depressive Disorder and common primary-care practice; educational summary.

## Assessment
Screen with PHQ-2/PHQ-9. Always ask about suicidal thoughts; active suicidal ideation with intent or plan is an emergency.

## Treatment
Psychotherapy (for example CBT) and/or an antidepressant. SSRIs such as sertraline (start 50 mg daily) or escitalopram are common first-line choices. Reassess response after 4 to 6 weeks; continue treatment for at least 6 to 12 months after remission.

## Safety
- Serotonin syndrome risk when SSRIs are combined with tramadol, triptans, linezolid, MAO inhibitors or other serotonergic drugs.
- SSRIs increase bleeding risk with NSAIDs, aspirin or anticoagulants.
- Hyponatremia risk in older adults.
- Monitor young adults for worsening suicidal thoughts early in treatment.
