# Hypertension in adults
Source: 2017 ACC/AHA Guideline for the Prevention, Detection, Evaluation, and Management of High Blood Pressure in Adults; educational summary.

## Classification
Normal below 120/80 mmHg; elevated 120-129/<80; stage 1 hypertension 130-139 or 80-89; stage 2 hypertension 140/90 or higher. Diagnosis should be based on an average of readings on separate occasions, ideally confirmed with home or ambulatory monitoring.

## Treatment
Lifestyle changes for all: weight loss, DASH-style diet, sodium reduction, potassium-rich diet, physical activity, limited alcohol. First-line drug classes: thiazide diuretics, ACE inhibitors, angiotensin receptor blockers (ARBs) and calcium channel blockers. Usual target below 130/80 mmHg for most adults with cardiovascular risk.

## Safety
- Do not combine an ACE inhibitor with an ARB.
- ACE inhibitors and ARBs raise potassium; monitor potassium and creatinine, especially with potassium-sparing diuretics, potassium supplements or trimethoprim.
- ACE inhibitors and ARBs are contraindicated in pregnancy.
- NSAIDs can raise blood pressure and reduce kidney function when combined with ACE inhibitors or ARBs and diuretics.

## Follow-up
Reassess monthly after starting or changing treatment until the target is reached.
