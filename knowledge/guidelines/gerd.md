# Gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD)
Source: ACG Clinical Guideline for the Diagnosis and Management of GERD (2022); educational summary.

## Treatment
For typical heartburn and regurgitation without alarm features, an 8-week trial of a once-daily proton pump inhibitor taken 30-60 minutes before a meal. Lifestyle measures: weight loss, avoiding late meals, raising the head of the bed. After symptoms resolve, try to reduce to the lowest effective dose or stop.

## Alarm features
Dysphagia, unintentional weight loss, GI bleeding, anemia or persistent vomiting need endoscopy.

## Safety
Long-term PPIs are associated with reduced absorption of some drugs and nutrients; omeprazole interacts with clopidogrel.
