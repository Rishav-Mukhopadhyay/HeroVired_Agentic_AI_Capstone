# Atrial fibrillation: stroke prevention
Source: 2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS Guideline for the Diagnosis and Management of Atrial Fibrillation; educational summary.

## Anticoagulation
Stroke risk is estimated with CHA2DS2-VASc. Oral anticoagulation is recommended when the score is 2 or more in men or 3 or more in women. Direct oral anticoagulants (apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran, edoxaban) are preferred over warfarin, except in patients with a mechanical heart valve or moderate-to-severe mitral stenosis, who need warfarin.

## Warfarin interactions
Many drugs increase warfarin's effect and bleeding risk, including trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole, metronidazole, fluconazole and some other antibiotics; INR should be checked closely when they are started. NSAIDs and antiplatelet drugs add bleeding risk with any anticoagulant.

## Rate and rhythm
Beta-blockers or non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers (not in HFrEF) for rate control; rhythm control considered for symptomatic patients and early in the disease.
